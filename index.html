How to Play

  • Move: drag or move side to side  (or ← → / A D)
  • Aim: point where you want to shoot
  • Shoot: tap or hold on the screen  (or hold SPACE)
  • Goal: blast the meteors before they smash the houses
  • Coins: each meteor you destroy pays coins — spend them in the shop on weapons, colors & keychains
  • Win: survive the waves to save Franks City!
